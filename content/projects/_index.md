**[Norns](https://nornscode.com)**. Durable execution for AI agents, built in Elixir. If an agent crashes mid-run, Norns replays its event log and picks up where it left off. Open source. [Read more](/posts/introducing-norns/) or see the [series](/norns/).

**[Mimir](https://nornscode.com/mimir)**. A Slack bot that answers product questions, built on Norns. It reads your repos, docs, Figma files, and URLs, and remembers what it learns. [Read more](/posts/introducing-mimir/).

**[Chronicle](https://chronicle.work)**. A shared history for recurring meetings. Never lose track of what was said in a 1:1 or team sync. [Read more](/posts/introducing-chronicle/).

**[Shotify](https://shotify.codes)**. Code screenshots from your editor. Emacs, VS Code, Neovim, CLI, and an npm package. [Read more](/posts/introducing-shotify/).

**[Float](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/float-meditation-timer/id6757253072)**. A meditation timer for iOS. [Read more](/posts/float-meditation-timer/).
