I create software systems that operate reliably under unreliable
conditions.

I've spent 15+ years building those systems and leading the teams
that build them. I co-founded [LegalMate](https://legalmate.co) and
spent four years as its CTO, ran engineering at [Ada](https://ada.cx)
and [Mangrove Systems](https://mangrovesystems.com), and helped build
[Hyper](https://callhyper.com), acquired by Motorola Solutions in
2026. Now I'm at [CoPlane](https://coplane.com), building AI agents
for the enterprise back office. The common thread is software that
isn't allowed to fail: 911 call handling, legal automation, carbon
accounting, finance operations.

I studied electrical and computer engineering at the University of
Waterloo, spent ten years in Toronto, and now live on Bowen Island,
a short ferry ride from Vancouver. I've shipped production code in
almost a dozen languages and built products used by millions. Full
history on my [CV](/cv).

## Start here

**Building.** [Norns](/posts/introducing-norns/) is an open-source
durable execution runtime for AI agents, built in Elixir on the BEAM.
If an agent crashes mid-run, it replays the event log and picks up
where it left off. The latest post in the series covers what crash
recovery gets wrong when agents delegate to other agents:
[Is This Still Happening?](/posts/is-this-still-happening/) All seven
posts are collected on the [Norns series page](/norns/).

**Leading.** [Being an Early Employee](/posts/being-an-early-employee/)
is the startup rocket ship metaphor, with more blast radius than
romance. Scaling yourself, failure vs success, and knowing when to
quit.

**Making.** Simple on purpose:
[Float](/posts/float-meditation-timer/) is a meditation timer with no
accounts or streaks, and [Chronicle](/posts/introducing-chronicle/)
gives recurring meetings a shared history. The rest live on the
[projects](/projects) page.
